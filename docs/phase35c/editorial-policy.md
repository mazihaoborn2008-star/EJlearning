# Phase 3.5C editorial policy

Chinese communicative meaning is the anchor. Each language selects its own natural lexical heads, senses, structures and complete examples. Structural symmetry is neither required nor inferred. Existing legacy records remain immutable; additive V2 records and a many-to-many crosswalk preserve their source IDs and decisions.

Vocabulary Stage concerns usefulness, semantic specificity and register. Grammar Level concerns productive structure and discourse demands. Expression vocabulary, grammar and overall difficulty receive separate judgments; interaction, ellipsis and pragmatic inference contribute to overall difficulty. Exam targets are separately authored, conservative curriculum references. All new alignment remains draft and cannot certify ability, official coverage or an internal-level equivalence.

This is model-assisted editorial review, not independent professional linguistic certification. A resolved migration decision means the content destination and semantic treatment have been decided; it does not mean a human linguist approved the content. Provenance records must identify that distinction explicitly.

Special decisions: thirsty can coexist with the Japanese state expression 喉が渇いた; Japanese explanatory phrases are not forcibly paired as lexical heads. English plan has separate noun and verb identities. Appointment in this curriculum means a scheduled meeting/service; Japanese 予約 only covers advance booking. Buffer is restricted to spare time. Pleasant anticipation is distinguished from prediction and requirements. Appearance そう uses observed signs, separately from hearsay. Counterfactual would have and past intention つもりだった are independently taught with completed contexts. Trailing request clauses are completed or explicitly situated in dialogue.

QA must report errors separately from editorial warnings and intentional omissions. Presence and span checks cannot establish semantic truth; authored links and source-by-source review remain necessary. No mastery, placement, recommendation, checkpoint, storage or AI changes are authorized. Only the new 35c Worker/D1 may be written remotely.
